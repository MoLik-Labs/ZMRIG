The 3 % fee, in full

zmrig mines 3 % of its Monero time for the developer. The other 97 % goes to the pool and wallet you configured. That is the whole policy:

Rate3 % of Monero (rx/0) mining time, --donate-level=0 to 10
Cadence60 s of Monero mining to the developer wallet after every 33 minutes of yours
CoinMonero only. The wallet is a Monero address, so ArQmA, Wownero, Graft, Safex, Yada and Scala time accrues nothing
Destinationthe pool you already use, with the developer wallet in the login. No second pool, no relay, no hidden endpoint
Switch--donate-level=0 or --no-donate turns it off completely

Nothing is taken from a share you solved: a pool pays whoever logs in, so the only way a miner can pay a fee without sitting in the middle of your traffic is to log in with the other wallet for a while. The clock is debt-based, so a reconnect does not lose or double the fee:

  • every second of Monero hashing adds 30 ms of debt at 3 % (100 ms at 10 %),
  • once 60 s is owed, the miner ends the current connection and reconnects on the same pool with the developer wallet,
  • to avoid a reconnect loop the cadence never exceeds one fee session per ~33 minutes at the default rate, and a pool that refuses the developer wallet postpones the fee by 2 minutes, then by 30 after three refusals, instead of interrupting your mining again.

Where it is printed​

It is not a silently applied setting. Every one of these says the rate and the address:

ZMRIG 6.36.0 - RandomX miner by @nggkhaiz
3% of Monero mining time goes to the developer (--donate-level=0 turns it off), GPL-3.0, https://github.com/MoLik-Labs/ZMRIG
zmrig ... --check | grep donate
#   donate  3% of Monero (rx/0) mining time to 46SQLW7DXaq6GJaQbHUFMAhd1VYpfQFJQ2gfZo9TCB8bf9vnf1MUwAD2Fwj6KMsY4RU3oKA3x6hByg8BKg7FfMWn6ysb5tJ, 60 s every 33 min (--donate-level=0 turns it off)

At runtime, once per session:

developer fee: 60 s owed, reconnecting to pay it
developer fee: login ok, mining job 7f3a… for the developer wallet
developer fee: session paid, back to your wallet

And the HTTP API reports "donate_level": 3 in /1/summary and /2/summary, so a dashboard cannot show zero while the miner is paying.

What the fee is not​

  • Not a cut of your shares. Nothing is submitted twice, nothing is re-weighted, the pool never sees a share for a wallet you did not choose outside the fee window.
  • Not payable in another coin. Mining ArQmA to a Monero address would burn the coins in a hole nobody can spend; the fee simply does not accrue there.
  • Not a hardcoded pool. A fee session uses the pool, port, TLS and proxy settings of the pool you configured. If that pool does not accept the developer wallet, the fee is postponed and your mining is untouched.

Check it yourself​

./scripts/verify-fee.sh --binary zmrig/out/zmrig

The script reads the fee constants, confirms --check prints the rate and the address, confirms --donate-level=0 prints off, and fails if a second donation host or a second wallet appears in the binary. Each check prints the line it read, so "it passed" is not the only output.

zmrig ... --check | grep donate           # the rate and the address
zmrig ... --donate-level=0                # nothing is mined for anyone but you
strings zmrig/out/zmrig | grep -c 46SQLW7D  # exactly one address

The C++ engine in this repo​

src/ is the upstream XMRig tree with every donation line removed, and it still has no fee at all (doc/ZERO_FEE.md, ./scripts/verify-fee-free.sh). It is kept for algorithms the Zig engine does not speak and can be selected with --engine=xmrig; the default engine, zmrig, is the one that pays the fee. If you want to support the work, staying on the default engine is what does it.

Why 3 %​

That is what the work costs, and a number that is stated can be argued with.

The fee pays for the parts that do not mine: CI builds and signs every image, the fleet and this site need a host and somebody to watch it, and the benchmarks behind these pages take a machine and a day each. At 1 % that is funded by the author's evenings; at 3 % a rig at 1 kH/s pays about a dollar a month, roughly the electricity of eight hours — small enough to leave a profitability calculation intact, large enough to keep the releases rolling.

It is not a corridor. --donate-level takes 0 through 10, the installer writes the value into config.json where you can read it, and scripts/verify-fee.sh fails the build if this page and the binary disagree.
